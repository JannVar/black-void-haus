About

The Void // Manifesto
BVH › About

Origin

Black Void Haus was born from the intersection of gothic ritual and high-mobility streetwear. Founded in 2023, the brand fuses archival darkness with modern technical construction — garments built for movement, designed for the abyss.

2023 Founded
48+ Archive Pieces
Global Shipping

Philosophy

Each collection is a document — an archive of darkness translated into wearable form. We design for those who exist at the edge of expression. Limited drops. No restocks. Once it's gone, it returns to the void.

Materials & Construction

All garments are constructed from premium heavyweight cotton, technical stretch fabrics, and custom-dyed materials. Every piece undergoes our signature wash process for that aged, lived-in feel from day one. We don't do fast fashion — we do archive fashion.

Drops

Black Void Haus operates on a seasonal drop model. Archive V1 launched Spring 2024. Archive V2 — the current collection — is live now. Subscribe to our transmissions for early access to upcoming drops.
